## ServiceCollectionExtensions\.AddCatEventProcessorInbox\(this IServiceCollection\) Method

Lets the receiver use the inbox of `Cat.Inbox` for the message types that have `UseInbox` on\.
An `ICatInboxStore` must be registered too, such as `AddCatInboxEfCore` of `Cat.Inbox.EFCore`\.
